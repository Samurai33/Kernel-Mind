## 🎵 Music Production Notes

### Mastering Standards
- **Target Loudness**: -16 LUFS (preserves dynamics)
- **Peak Levels**: -6dBFS maximum
- **Dynamic Range**: Minimum 8 LU maintained
- **Sample Rate**: 48kHz native (no upsampling artifacts)

### Binaural Implementation
- **Carrier Frequency**: 220Hz (A3) for optimal perception
- **Beat Frequencies**: Scientifically selected based on research
- **Modulation**: Subtle LFO to prevent habituation
- **Spatialization**: Proper L/R channel implementation

### Quality Assurance
- All tracks professionally mastered
- Multiple format testing (FLAC, MP3, AAC)
- Headphone compatibility verification
- Long-session listening tests
